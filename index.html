About the school

Ofua's government-aided secondary school

Ofua Secondary School is located in Ofua, Adjumani District, in Uganda's West Nile region. Government records list the school under EMIS code 418002.

Uganda Ministry of Education placement information lists the institution as Ofua Seed School, with selection code 1008.

The same Ministry information classifies the school as O-Level, mixed and day. Its published postal address is P.O. Box 146, Adjumani.

Older education and directory sources sometimes use the shorter name Ofua S.S. or Ofua Secondary School. Current government and sports-school records commonly use Ofua Seed Secondary School.

Naming note: This profile treats Ofua S.S., Ofua Secondary School and Ofua Seed Secondary School as references to the same institution because the government records connect the Ofua S.S. EMIS code 418002 with current Ofua Seed Secondary School records.

Facilities & development

Infrastructure supporting learners

Government and development-project records document several infrastructure and service improvements at Ofua Seed Secondary School.

ICT

ICT facilities

Adjumani district ICT records list Ofua Seed Secondary School among institutions receiving computer equipment. The records specifically report 10 computers associated with the school and identify the school in the district's ICT-in-education programme.

🏠

Student dormitories

A 2023 DRDIP Uganda report documented student dormitories constructed at Ofua Seed School through the Development Response to Displacement Impacts Project.

💧

Water infrastructure

Adjumani District and Uganda's Ministry of Water and Environment records document work connected with water services at Ofua Seed Secondary School, including the design of a piped water system.

☀

National ICT infrastructure records list Ofua S.S. Seed among schools supported through solar-powered school ICT projects.

🚲

2026 bicycle programme

Ofua Seed Secondary School was one of five Adjumani government grant-aided secondary schools reported to have received bicycles under a 2026 school-access programme.

2026 development

52 bicycles for school access

Ofua Seed Secondary School was among the beneficiary schools in an Adjumani initiative designed to reduce transportation barriers affecting learners.

🚲

52 bicycles

Ofua Seed Secondary School received 52 bicycles: 50 intended for learners and two for school administration.

🧑‍🎓

Learner transport

The programme was designed to help learners facing long journeys between home and school, reducing lateness and absenteeism.

🛠️

Maintenance support

The wider bicycle programme includes bicycle maintenance tools and training intended to support continued use of the bicycles.

🌧️

Weather challenges

Reporting on the programme notes that long journeys and poor weather can contribute to learners arriving late or missing school.

🇺🇬

Government grant-aided school

Current reporting describes Ofua Seed Secondary School as one of the government grant-aided secondary schools participating in the initiative.

🌍

Refugee-hosting district

The programme is part of wider efforts in Adjumani to improve access to education for learners in refugee-hosting communities.

Community

Education in Ofua and Adjumani

Ofua is part of Adjumani District's wider rural secondary education network and the district's refugee-hosting environment.

A school serving a rural community

Government records place Ofua Seed Secondary School in Ofua Subcounty, Adjumani District. District planning documents identify the school within the local government secondary education system.

Development programmes have also targeted the school because of infrastructure and access challenges faced by learners in the area.

The 2023 DRDIP reporting on the school's dormitories described health and environmental challenges affecting learners, while more recent government water records document efforts to improve water infrastructure at the school.
